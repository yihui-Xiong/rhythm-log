# Rhythm Log portfolio assets

Every image is an iPhone simulator capture with synthetic records. The gallery includes both a daytime-only profile and one with overnight records. The files are organized in walkthrough order.

[Open the gallery](index.html) · [Project description](project-description.md)

## Getting started

- [Welcome and Apple Health access](screenshots/01-welcome.png) — The first-run screen introduces Day, Training, and Recovery, explains that sleep is optional, and opens Apple Health’s read-permission flow.

## Day timeline and readings

- [24-hour timeline](screenshots/02-day-timeline.png) — A full day of synthetic HRV, sleep, and activity. Hold the dial to inspect an hourly estimate; use the cards below to open related readings.
- [How the HRV score works](screenshots/03-score-guide.png) — The in-app guide explains the personal comparison and its separate daytime and overnight reference histories.
- [Daytime HRV and its reference band](screenshots/04-day-hrv.png) — See individual SDNN measurements in milliseconds and the personal reference shading. The pressure timeline groups readings by hour.
- [30-day HRV history](screenshots/05-hrv-history.png) — Open history from the day chart to inspect daily medians, data coverage, and dates with no readings.
- [Individual HealthKit readings](screenshots/06-raw-readings.png) — Open the record list to check the timestamp, value, and source behind plotted measurements.
- [Hourly activity](screenshots/07-day-steps.png) — The Day view groups steps into hourly totals. The same section leads to the sleep and workout blocks when records exist.
- [Sleep stages and raw intervals](screenshots/08-sleep-stages.png) — Color bands show recorded sleep stages. Expand the list to inspect individual start and end times.
- [Workouts on the day timeline](screenshots/09-day-workouts.png) — A workout row shows activity, time, duration, and active energy when recorded. It opens the same detail screen as the training report.

## Workout detail

- [Workout summary](screenshots/10-workout-overview.png) — View duration, active energy, heart-rate statistics, and available recovery. The header gives the workout’s position among sessions of that type.
- [Time in heart-rate zones](screenshots/11-workout-zones.png) — Bars show accounted-for time and BPM boundaries. The app labels recorded zone time or estimates based on adjacent readings.
- [Heart-rate chart during a workout](screenshots/12-workout-heart-rate.png) — Switch between Raw and Color-coded readings, inspect heart rate across the session, and open the underlying samples.
- [Workout frequency and month calendar](screenshots/13-workout-calendar.png) — The workout detail highlights other days with the same activity. Select a day to open its workout list.

## Training analysis

- [Weekly training volume](screenshots/14-week-training.png) — Eight rolling seven-day blocks show recorded minutes by sport. The selected block compares total minutes and highlights the largest sport-level change.
- [Weekly workout drill-down](screenshots/15-week-workouts.png) — Expand the list below a selected bar to review the workouts that contribute to that block.
- [Pace versus heart rate and one-minute recovery](screenshots/16-training-performance.png) — Choose an activity and setting. Compare average heart rate at similar paces and review recorded post-workout recovery on a separate chart.
- [Monthly training calendar](screenshots/17-training-calendar.png) — Each day can show a main activity and total workout minutes. Tap a day to open its workouts.

## Daytime and overnight recovery

- [Daytime HRV without sleep data](screenshots/18-recovery-no-sleep.png) — This sample profile has no sleep records. Daily HRV, the seven-day median, the prior range, and recorded same-day context still populate.
- [Resting heart rate and daily steps](screenshots/19-recovery-activity.png) — Compare coverage and medians with the previous period. These two series also work with no sleep history.
- [Sleep timing by wake date](screenshots/20-sleep-timing.png) — When sleep exists, select a night to review its time span, sleep duration, overnight HRV, and wake-date resting heart rate.
- [Daytime and overnight HRV](screenshots/21-overnight-hrv.png) — The Overnight view uses a separate series and baseline. Gaps remain visible when the app lacks enough measurements.
- [Sleep heart rate and respiratory rate](screenshots/22-overnight-vitals.png) — Charts use a shared date axis and metric-specific units and personal ranges. Select a night to inspect its values.
- [Blood oxygen and wrist temperature](screenshots/23-oxygen-and-wrist-temperature.png) — Available measurements appear in their own units. Gaps and values outside the prior personal range are visible.

## Calendars, navigation, and preferences

- [Monthly recovery calendar](screenshots/24-recovery-calendar.png) — Use the metric menu to switch between HRV score, HRV, heart rate, and resting heart rate. Tap a date for the Day view.
- [Yearly heart trends](screenshots/25-year-trends.png) — Monthly aggregates show longer-term patterns in heart rate, HRV, and resting heart rate.
- [Date picker](screenshots/26-date-picker.png) — Jump to a date from the date heading, then use Day, Week, Month, or Year to change the view.
- [Language, appearance, and Health access](screenshots/27-settings.png) — Settings include English and Simplified Chinese, display preferences, manual zones, and a way to update Apple Health permissions.
- [Manual zone boundaries](screenshots/28-manual-zones.png) — Enter four increasing BPM boundaries when your preferred HealthKit zone settings are unavailable. The values shown here are synthetic.
