# Rhythm Log

**iOS health data visualization · SwiftUI, Swift Charts, HealthKit**

I built Rhythm Log to make Apple Health data easier to explore in context. An interactive 24-hour dial aligns heart readings with workouts and, when recorded, sleep. Weekly and monthly views connect training volume with workout detail, heart-rate zones, and pace comparisons. The recovery view is designed for people who take off their watch at night. Daytime HRV, resting heart rate, and steps remain useful on their own, with overnight views available when records exist.

I designed the app around the limits of wearable data. Personal reference ranges come with coverage counts, missing measurements stay blank, and the interface distinguishes measured values from estimates. The project combines native iOS development with an evidence-informed approach to showing uncertainty in personal health data.
